# Hypotheses & Ideas Ledger

Open questions and claims-not-yet-tested, numbered and citable — the
forward-looking twin of [LEARNINGS.md](./LEARNINGS.md), which records what we
have already established.

A learning is something we know. This file holds the things we don't yet — and
they are not all the same kind of thing, so the **Kind** column says which:

- **hypothesis** — a claim that could be shown wrong. It names, or can name, a
  result that would refute it. H-002 and H-010 are already marked refuted, which
  is the column doing its job.
- **idea** — a design consideration, a principle, or a proposed method we are
  carrying as we go. Not a claim, not falsifiable as stated, and not pretending
  to be. Most of these are good ideas that simply are not predictions.

**An idea becomes a hypothesis the moment someone states what result would
refute it.** That is the only promotion criterion, and it is the same discipline
H-008 asks for everywhere else.

Both get an ID, because an idea that only exists in a conversation is one that
will be re-derived from scratch in three months, usually worse. Bigger ideas —
whole programmes rather than single considerations — live in
[experiment-ideas.md](../experiment-ideas.md) as lettered tracks instead.

**Statuses:** **open** (stated, untested) · **testing** (an experiment is
running against it) · **supported** (evidence for, cite the entry) ·
**refuted** (evidence against, cite the entry) · **superseded by H-###**.

Refuted hypotheses are never deleted. Being wrong in a specific, recorded way
is worth more than being vaguely right.

## Attribution

Every hypothesis carries its origin, because on a project run by a person and
a model together, *who thought of it* is part of the record and cannot be
reconstructed afterwards. See the attribution rule in
[README.md](./README.md#attribution-who-thought-of-it).

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[J]** | Javid's, in substance. Stated by him, not prompted by the model. |
| **[C]** | Claude's, in substance. Originated by the model. |
| **[J→C]** | Javid seeded it; Claude developed, formalised or operationalised it. |
| **[C→J]** | Claude proposed it; Javid selected, redirected or decided on it. |

Ideas that arose in conversation between Javid and someone else are recorded as
**[J]** with the source conversation cited, rather than given a third mark —
his call, and the honest one when a transcript is not diarised and turns cannot
be recovered.

| H-001 | **[J]** | **idea** | **Intelligence is transfer and retention, not task accuracy.** A system that learns task A, then learns task B, and still performs A, is intelligent in a way that a system with higher accuracy on A alone is not. Accuracy on a single fixed task is a benchmark measure, and benchmarks are what this project explicitly is not for. | open | experiment 003 (transfer/retention), design owed |
| H-002 | **[J]** | **hypothesis** | **A living system may learn on timescales far longer than we have been observing.** A flat learning curve over N trials is not evidence of no learning; it may be evidence of an observation window chosen for convenience. A system that took a month of wall-clock to become competent, and then generalised, would be more interesting than one that converges in 800 trials. | **refuted for 002's M1 arm** (scope: this substrate, 128× the original window — every measure flat, persistence 5% throughout); open as a general principle | [2026-08-22-1215](./entries/2026-08-22-1215-longrun-and-a-correction.md) |
| H-003 | **[C]** | **hypothesis** | **Slow learning requires a slow variable.** A system in which every state variable has a short time constant cannot learn slowly — it reaches steady state and then stays there, and further time buys only more steady state. So H-002 is not a claim about patience; it is a claim about mechanism, and it is checkable by asking what the longest-lived quantity in the system is. | open | measure the timescale of every state variable in 002; compare against observed persistence |
| H-004 | **[C]** | **hypothesis** | **002's only genuinely accumulating variable is stored on its most ephemeral object.** The evidence count `n` only ever increases (design §7), which makes it the one monotone quantity in the substrate — and it lives on edges, ~94% of which die every generation. The slow variable is destroyed along with its carrier, so nothing accumulates across sleeps. Moving it to the *site* — a per-location trace that outlives the edges that created it and biases where regrowth goes — would give the substrate its first long-term memory. | open | a site-trace variant of 002, measured by structural persistence |
| H-005 | **[J→C]** | **hypothesis** | **Savings detects learning that behaviour cannot show.** A substrate may hold a latent trace it does not express. Re-teaching a previously-taught task should then be faster than teaching it to a naive control, *even if accuracy during the first exposure never left chance*. This is Ebbinghaus's method, not an ML method, and it is the right instrument for H-002: it can find learning inside a flat curve. | open — **first attempt void**: 003 measured savings with a floor at the rolling-window length, so `T_A2`=100 on 5/5 seeds is a censored value, not a duration (L-032). Needs a re-run without the floor | savings protocol on 001 and 002, with a never-taught control arm |
| H-006 | **[C]** | **hypothesis** | **Growth is activity-seeking but not correlation-seeking.** A node grows toward wherever there is activity, regardless of whether that activity has anything to do with its own. So each interior node accumulates ~5 mutually unrelated inputs and averages them into noise, which is a mechanism for L-013's one-hop wall. Growth that preferred *correlated* targets would build coherent receptive fields instead of random mixtures. | open | a correlation-seeking growth rule, measured by hop-2 decodability |
| H-007 | **[J→C]** | **idea** | **Detection of learning should be a ladder, not a threshold.** Behaviour is the last thing to change, so a single behavioural gate cannot distinguish "no machinery" from "machinery that has not yet reached behaviour". Evidence should be sought at every level from structure upward, with each level having its own null. | open | the ladder in [vision.md](../vision.md#how-we-judge-whether-it-is-learning) |
| H-008 | **[C]** | **idea** | **Unfalsifiability is this project's real occupational hazard.** "It is learning, just slowly and invisibly" is unanswerable unless every new instrument ships with a null and a pre-registered decision rule. H-002 and H-005 are worth pursuing *and* are exactly the shape of belief that quietly turns a research project into a faith. The defence is that no instrument enters the record without stating what result would count as no-learning. | open | applies to every entry; enforced by journal rule 3 |
| H-009 | **[J]** | **idea** | **A measure of learning is admissible only if it is substrate-independent.** If it could be applied to an ant, an ape, a human and a machine, it is measuring learning; if it only makes sense for a system with a loss function, a train/test split and epochs, it is measuring a *training procedure* and may not transfer to a living model. Borrowed measures are fine — most of comparative psychology qualifies, most of the ML evaluation battery does not. | open | applied as an admission test to every rung in [vision.md](../vision.md#what-makes-a-measure-admissible) |
| H-010 | **[J→C]** | **hypothesis** | **Latent learning is the decisive test for H-002.** Expose the organism with reward switched off, then switch reward on, and compare trials-to-criterion against a naive control. If pre-exposure helps, it was acquiring structure the whole time while behaviour showed nothing — which is exactly the claim "it might be learning invisibly" makes. Tolman's 1930 rats did precisely this. It converts an unfalsifiable worry into a controlled experiment with a control group. | **refuted** — no pattern-specific latent learning on either substrate; the scrambled control matched or beat the pre-exposed arm, so the speed-up is warm-up (L-024) | [2026-08-22-1240](./entries/2026-08-22-1240-latent-learning.md) |
| H-011 | **[C]** | **idea** | **Trials-to-criterion is the right currency, not accuracy.** "How many exposures to reach a fixed standard" is comparable across systems with different asymptotes and speeds; "accuracy at trial 2,000" is comparable to nothing, and silently penalises a slow learner for being slow rather than for failing. Every gate from 003 onward should be written in trials-to-criterion. | open | rewrite the 003 gates in this currency and check it reproduces 001's and 002's known results |
| H-012 | **[J→C]** | **hypothesis** | **Scaffolded growth: crystallisation needs positive feedback from what has already formed to what forms next.** 002 builds a strong, well-evidenced core and it never grows, because the growth rule cannot see it (L-028). Make growth's target weighting depend on consolidated structure — `A(target)·exp(−span/λ_g)·(1 + κ·evidence near target)` — so a core that forms becomes a frame later growth follows. Javid's clause "when it gets strong enough, faster things can grow around it" has no mechanism in the current design. | open | pre-registered measure: **the core must grow across checkpoints**, which it demonstrably does not today |
| H-013 | **[J]** | **idea** | **Infinite reward, limited punishment — and punishment is death.** Positive reinforcement should be unbounded; negative reinforcement accumulates against a threshold, and crossing it **kills the instance** rather than adjusting a weight. 002 already does exactly this one level down — rent is a bounded running cost and failure to pay is death — so the proposal is to lift a mechanism that already works at the synapse up to the organism. | open | organism-level death on accumulated punishment, measured against the current symmetric advantage rule · [source]([2026-08-22-1340](./entries/2026-08-22-1340-kian-conversation.md)) |
| H-014 | **[J]** | **hypothesis** | **Inheritance is the accumulator: the ultimate reward is passing on what was learned.** A successful instance seeds the next with part of what it acquired. This attacks [L-028](./LEARNINGS.md)'s gap from the opposite side to H-012 — where that adds within-lifetime scaffolding, this adds across-lifetime transmission, and a population under selection also puts the *rules* under selection, which today's [knob table](../experiments/002-grown-substrate/design.md) notes they currently are not. | open | a population of instances with partial inheritance; measure whether generation N reaches criterion faster than generation 1 · [source]([2026-08-22-1340](./entries/2026-08-22-1340-kian-conversation.md)) |
| H-015 | **[J]** | **idea** | **How much is pre-programmed is a dial, not a binary.** A spider spins a web untaught; humans look unprogrammed but have balance, breathing and blinking built in. 001 is fully given, 002 is fully grown, and the interesting region is between them. This also answers the standing worry about whether defining what a system should learn is cheating: **an organism's parameters are built in too — having a genome is not cheating.** | open | a series of substrates varying the given/grown ratio, measured on the same battery · [source]([2026-08-22-1340](./entries/2026-08-22-1340-kian-conversation.md)) |
| H-016 | **[J]** | **idea** | **Structure-versus-noise is the first test of pattern learning, and it is not a classification task.** Show 100 images of which 80 are random dots and 20 have real structure, and ask whether it can separate them *without being told what the structure is*. A system that cannot tell signal from noise has not begun; one that can has done something no label taught it. | open | 80 noise / 20 structured images on 001 and 002; chance is the base rate · [source]([2026-08-22-1340](./entries/2026-08-22-1340-kian-conversation.md)) |
| H-017 | **[J]** | **idea** | **Learning and measurement are separate phases: test with games it was never trained on.** Train on one thing, then run a battery of *novel* games — horizontal versus vertical, which image has more lines, find the longest line — and measure how fast it adapts to each. Stated independently of, and converging with, H-011 (trials-to-criterion) and ladder rung 7 (Harlow's learning set): "you've learned a bunch of stuff in school but at the end I'm going to throw you into a whole set of challenges." | open | the game battery, scored in trials-to-criterion per novel game · [source]([2026-08-22-1340](./entries/2026-08-22-1340-kian-conversation.md)) |
| H-018 | **[J]** | **idea** | **Perception and communication are separable competencies, and a rule flip is a *protocol* change, not a knowledge change.** A smart system identifies what it sees once, then independently learns how to *communicate* what it found under whatever protocol is in force — so it should not crater on a flip; it should re-bind cheaply. Two admissible presentations of a new protocol: **shown** (a rules cortex — the expected mapping arrives through a sense) and **inferred** (figure it out from reward alone, unannounced). These are different challenges and both belong in the battery; inferred is already measured (001 fails flat, L-033), shown is not. | open | experiment 005 (cued protocol switching), design owed |
| H-019 | **[J]** | **idea** | **The dual axis: vary the inputs, hold the protocol.** Rotate or zoom the stimuli while the communication rule stays fixed — perceptual invariance as the complement of protocol flexibility. With H-018 this forms a 2×2 (percept varies / protocol varies), and the far cell — both at once — is the real target. Input variation kin to backlog §G. | open | future experiment, after 005 |
| H-020 | **[J→C]** | **hypothesis** | **002 fails because it occupies a point in design space no animal occupies: zero inherited routing + unstructured spontaneous noise + refinement-only growth.** Biology divides the labour — the genome routes coarse topology (protomap, guidance-molecule gradients), structured spontaneous activity (retinal waves) carries the correlations, and Hebbian growth only *refines*. 002 asked the refinement mechanism to do the routing job on white noise. Prediction: an inherited laminar affinity scaffold + structured waves + correlation-seeking growth yields multi-hop information transmission where 50 knob variations yielded none (L-040). | open | experiment 006, staged arms with each component ablated |
| H-021 | **[C]** | **idea** | **Spontaneous activity must be *structured* to instruct.** 002's `pSpont` is iid white noise, which carries zero correlations for growth to read; retinal waves are travelling correlated fronts whose *pattern* is the information (disrupt the pattern, not the amount, and maps fail). If growth is to learn topography before reward exists, the bootstrap activity must have topology in it. | open | wave-generator on the sense during development, vs iid control |
| H-022 | **[C]** | **idea** | **Development is a schedule, not a steady state.** Biology sequences regimes — exuberant overproduction, then pruning; high plasticity, then gated closure (critical periods, closed *deliberately* and reopenably, unlike L-034's accidental terminal freeze). 002 runs one stationary regime for its whole life. Growth rate, rent and plasticity should be functions of developmental time. | smallest form **refuted as implemented** (rent-free childhood floods the sheet, L-044); the schedule idea stays open in selection-preserving forms | staged schedules in 006; the no-schedule arm is the control |
| H-023 | **[C]** | **idea** | **Wires and weights need different lifetimes.** One object currently carries both a connection's existence and its strength, so weight-level bookkeeping (rent) destroys structure-level capital (L-042). Split them: tracts persist on a long timescale (or earn durability with evidence — myelination), while synaptic weight along a tract stays cheap and plastic. | **supported** (one-line form) — 002-M1g: first rising multi-seed learning on the hard geometry, L-043 | tract/weight separation in 006; the conflated model is the control |
| H-024 | **[C]** | **idea** | **Fasciculation: new growth follows existing tracts.** 002's growth is bounded by rMax, so a dead long edge is gone forever. Biology regrows long wiring along existing axon bundles. Let a growth cone extend along a live edge's path (or chain onto its endpoint) so long-range structure is renewable. | open | 006 arm; measure: long-edge population is stable instead of monotonically decaying |
| H-025 | **[J→C]** | **idea** | **Knobs as per-unit random variables, heritable.** Every parameter is currently one global constant shared by 1,024 neurons; biology has distributions everywhere (excitability, growth vigor, reach, synaptic strength, stabilization propensity vary per cell/synapse). Draw them per-unit from ranges, and let the scaffold-selection loop inherit and mutate the *distributions* — putting the physics under selection, which design §11 notes it currently is not. Seeded by Javid's question: which knobs make sense as a bayesian probability within a range? | open | per-unit variation arms after the confirmation stage |
| H-026 | **[J]** | **hypothesis** | **First-exposure competence is the adaptability currency, not trials-to-criterion.** "The measure isn't how fast it can train through thousands of iterations — it can come in to a new, yet similar activity and be pretty good right from the start." Trials-to-criterion (H-011) is a *rate*; this is an *intercept*, and a system can have a good rate with a catastrophic intercept — 001 under a rule flip starts **below chance** (L-030) yet eventually reaches criterion. Refuted if first-block accuracy on novel games turns out to be rank-equivalent to trials-to-criterion across entrants, i.e. the two currencies never disagree. Corollary, inherited from §H's scoring argument: the two are reported side by side and never collapsed into one number. | open | the game battery (H-017 / §H), scored on both currencies · [source](./entries/2026-08-26-2341-adaptability-the-measure.md) |
| H-027 | **[J→C]** | **hypothesis** | **Amortised adaptation is distinguishable from earned adaptation, by held-out *family* rather than held-out *task*.** Meta-RL, domain randomisation and AdA all buy fast adaptation in advance with coverage of the task family, and degrade outside it. So a fast-adapting entrant is not evidence of the mechanism this project is after unless the game lies outside the family it trained on — which makes held-out family the load-bearing control in §H, and makes "what family was this trained over" a required entry declaration alongside provenance. Refuted if an entrant's adaptation speed is *flat* across in-family and out-of-family games, in which case the distinction is doing no work. **Named risk:** this is the shape of argument that lets a project dismiss every competing result as not-really-the-same-thing; it stays a framing until run. | open | §H design, when §H is designed · [source](./entries/2026-08-26-2341-adaptability-the-measure.md) |
| H-028 | **[J→C]** | **hypothesis** | **"Living" must beat its own control, and the control is a short freeze.** The claimed benefits of being always-on — no distribution shift, no retraining cadence, no fine-tuning regression, adaptation with no task-family boundary — are only benefits if a **conventional model retrained from scratch on everything-so-far, on a schedule** cannot match them on the same battery. That control has a freeze; it just has a short one. Refuted if the periodically-retrained arm matches the living organism on acquisition *and* retention, in which case "living" is an implementation convenience rather than a property and the theoretical case for it is aesthetic. Required arm in any experiment claiming a benefit *from* always-on operation. | open | the retrained control arm, on whatever battery §H eventually becomes · [source](./entries/2026-08-27-0000-is-a-living-ai-novel.md) |
| H-029 | **[J]** | **hypothesis** | **A perceivable world clock buys three things a stationary world cannot.** Give the world a slow periodic phase and let the organism *see* it (one cell advancing every N ticks, as a **second sense**, not extra eye pixels). (a) It attacks **H-003** from the world's side: the organism need not *hold* a long timescale, only **read** one — long-horizon structure becomes a perceptual problem rather than a memory problem, and on the record this substrate is far better at perception than memory (L-042). (b) Rule context becomes **ambient** rather than instructed — 005's cue with the announcement removed, strictly harder than 005 and strictly easier than the unannounced flip 001 fails flat (L-033). (c) Timing games become expressible at all — duration, delay, peak-interval — and interval timing passes H-009 trivially (pigeons, rats, monkeys, humans; scalar timing theory, Gibbon 1977). Refuted, per component, if a **constant-clock control arm** matches the running-clock arm: a result that appears either way means the clock is decoration. | **superseded by H-031** — the three things it claims all survive; its *mechanism* does not. A dedicated clock sense is an instruction, not a phenomenon: if time arrives on its own channel you can only ever measure whether the organism reads a wire, never whether it *built* a sense of time [J] | [2026-08-27-0040](./entries/2026-08-27-0040-the-sun.md) |
| H-030 | **[J]** | **hypothesis** | **Separate the organs and you get the factoring for free.** Give the body one channel that *acts on the world* (a hand, which writes to the grid the eye sees) and a different channel that *communicates about it* (a mouth, off-grid, whose only job is the answer). H-018 hoped perception and communication would prove separable competencies inside one substrate; this makes the separation **anatomical** rather than hypothetical — it stops being something the network must discover. Refuted if an organism whose single output channel serves both roles matches the split-organ body on protocol-flip and rule-cue games, in which case the anatomy buys nothing and H-018's factoring must come from the substrate after all. | open | §I v0, once a game exists that needs both organs · [source](./entries/2026-08-27-0023-the-world.md) |
| H-031 | **[J]** | **hypothesis** | **The Sun: time is a property of the world, never a channel into the organism.** "The clock is a property of the world, like the Sun. It has to produce some change in the world and give a mechanism for the AI to develop a reference frame. But time itself cannot be internal to the AI." Mechanism: a sun occupies a position, **illuminates a band**, and advances; outside the band the world reads dark. Time becomes inferable from where the light is and from what is currently invisible — and **knowing the time costs a glance**, with no free channel. This adds a **third** category to H-018's shown/inferred pair: *inferred from a perceptible world regularity*, which is how animals actually do it and which was missing from the project's map. Refuted if organisms in a sunlit world show no time-dependent behaviour that organisms in a uniformly-lit world of the same difficulty do not — i.e. if the illumination is only ever an obstacle and never a cue. | open | §I; the sun as the first world property, before any motor · [source](./entries/2026-08-27-0040-the-sun.md) |
| H-032 | **[J→C]** | **hypothesis** | **Free-running: an organism in a rhythmic world grows the slow variable it was never given.** Entrain behaviour to the sun, then remove it — hold the world in constant conditions — and ask whether timed behaviour **persists and drifts** rather than stopping. This is the protocol run on every organism from cyanobacteria to humans (Aschoff), it passes H-009 without argument, and it is the sharpest available test of **H-003**: a persisting rhythm means the organism *developed* a long time constant from an external regularity instead of being handed one — which is precisely what L-042 showed it could not do when the timescale had to live on its own ephemeral structure. Refuted if behaviour collapses immediately to the un-entrained baseline the moment the sun is removed (masking, not entrainment). **The rejected clock-sense design made this experiment impossible**, which is the strongest argument for H-031. | open | after H-031; constant-conditions arm vs sunlit arm, same seeds · [source](./entries/2026-08-27-0040-the-sun.md) |
| H-033 | **[C]** | **hypothesis** | **The world's night and the organism's sleep should be the same event.** 002 already confines all structural change to an offline sleep phase, chosen as an implementation convenience. A sun gives the world a night. If sleep is *entrained* to the world's dark phase rather than fired on an arbitrary tick schedule, restructuring happens exactly when there is nothing to perceive and nothing to lose — and §H's scoring section already wants sleep as a manipulable condition rather than a hidden detail. Refuted if entrained sleep matches arbitrary-schedule sleep on the same battery, in which case the alignment is aesthetic. Note the confound to control: entrainment changes sleep *timing* and may change its *frequency*, so the arms must be matched on total sleep count. | open | §I, once the sun exists and 002's cadence work (L-045) is settled · [source](./entries/2026-08-27-0040-the-sun.md) |
| H-034 | **[J]** | **hypothesis** | **Light is the primitive and the room is the unit; time-legibility is a property of the place.** A room is contents + a light + a game; a light is geometry × motion × intensity × falloff. A light that **moves** carries time, a light with a **gradient** carries space, a uniform static light carries neither and is the honest control for both. The payoff is methodological: an organism moved from a `sunlit` room to a `windowless` one is **in constant conditions without anything being switched off** — **H-032**'s free-running test becomes a *transition between two rooms that each exist for their own reasons*, rather than an experimenter intervention on the world. Refuted if behaviour in `sunlit` is indistinguishable from `windowless` at matched difficulty, i.e. if a moving light is only ever an obstacle and never a cue. **Discipline attached (L-040):** rooms are *enumerated*, never swept — adding one requires naming the game that could not be played without it. | open | §I / experiment 007 · [source](./entries/2026-08-27-0100-rooms.md) |
| H-035 | **[J→C]** | **hypothesis** | **Intensity is firing probability, and a dim room is the first task where looking longer should pay.** The eye stays 64 binary pixels and light intensity becomes the probability that an illuminated cell fires this tick — which changes no interface (so recorded results still reproduce), fits a substrate that is *already* stochastic, and makes full illumination p=1.0 exactly the recorded behaviour.
