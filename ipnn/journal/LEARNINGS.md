| L-001 | Reward-modulated three-factor learning (score-function eligibility × broadcast advantage) is sufficient for 3-pattern discrimination at toy scale — no backprop, no labels. | L-002 | Softmax saturation kills score-function learning: un-normalized summed drives push one output's p→1, making eligibility (fired − p)→0, so the dominant answer can never be punished — irrecoverable answer collapse. | L-003 | Persistent exploration is load-bearing, not a nicety: ε-mixed sampling (ε=0.05, with the mixed probability used in eligibility so it matches the policy) is required for non-dominant outputs to ever claim patterns. | active | [2026-08-16-0248](./entries/2026-08-16-0248-experiment-001-m0-m1-first-build.md) |
| L-004 | Confidence–plasticity tension: the mechanism behind answer collapse (high confidence → zero learning signal) is the same mechanism IPNN deliberately uses for consolidation. "Consolidated memory" and "frozen wrong answer" are one phenomenon with different valence; expect this tension at every scale. | active | [2026-08-16-0248](./entries/2026-08-16-0248-experiment-001-m0-m1-first-build.md) |
| L-005 | Frozen retention holds at toy scale: after 500 rewarded trials, 100 trials with learning off scored 0.97 — behavior persists without ongoing reward (the living-model claim, design §1 step 7, verified small). | active | [2026-08-16-0248](./entries/2026-08-16-0248-experiment-001-m0-m1-first-build.md) |
| L-006 | Diagnostic heuristic: accuracy pinned at *exactly* chance across seeds signals deterministic collapse (always answering, always the same way), not noise. Noise wanders; collapse pins. | active | [2026-08-16-0248](./entries/2026-08-16-0248-experiment-001-m0-m1-first-build.md) |
| L-007 | Process: the headless gate-first sequencing (M1 before any UI) caught a paradigm-level defect in a <2s test loop. Instrument-then-diagnose beat guess-then-tune: one instrumented run identified collapse that hyperparameter twiddling would have chased for hours. | active | [2026-08-16-0248](./entries/2026-08-16-0248-experiment-001-m0-m1-first-build.md) |
| L-008 | An instrument must not be dimensioned by its own readings. Live-updating text (a label that changes phrase, a number that gains a digit) must be barred from sizing its container — reserve the space instead — or the panel physically moves in time with the process it is measuring. Applies to every panel the M2/M3 demos inherit. | active | [2026-08-21-0008](./entries/2026-08-21-0008-demo-layout-jitter-fix.md) |
| L-009 | A commit-once readout cannot measure a mind changing. The teacher's "spoken" rule ends the exposure at the first threshold crossing, making revision unobservable by construction — the temporal-dynamics track needed a free-running readout before it needed any new mechanism. Generalizes: when the question is about a process, check the instrument can represent more than one outcome per trial. | active | [2026-08-21-0025](./entries/2026-08-21-0025-manual-mode-sustained-readout.md) |
| L-010 | Experiment 001's hidden pool contributes nothing *through its plasticity*: freezing 89% of the learnable synapses (`etaPool=0`) changes tail accuracy by 0.004 — an order of magnitude below seed spread. The M1 result is a 480-weight readout on a **fixed random projection**. Corollary for all future comparisons: the baseline to beat is a random projection, not chance. | active | [2026-08-21-0145](./entries/2026-08-21-0145-m1b-etapool-ablation.md) |
| L-011 | Deriving a design from prior art produces prior art. Working the real system's constraints first, and consulting the literature only as an after-the-fact check on claims, surfaced mechanisms a literature-first pass did not. Prior art is a check on claims, not a source of designs. | active | [2026-08-21-0050](./entries/2026-08-21-0050-novelty-audit-and-the-missing-i.md) |
| L-012 | Passing gates is not implementing the specification. Every M1 gate passed while the mechanism the project is *named for* — iterative consideration via output→input feedback — was absent, because gates test what was built, not what was specified. Spec-versus-source audits belong on a schedule. | active | [2026-08-21-0050](./entries/2026-08-21-0050-novelty-audit-and-the-missing-i.md) |
| L-013 | In a sparse grown substrate of stochastic binary units under homeostatic inhibition, **stimulus information survives exactly one hop**: 7.7σ mean discriminability at hop 1, then 1.7–1.9σ at every hop beyond, against a null expectation of exactly 3/√π = 1.693 — the distal substrate carries no signal at all, not a weak one. Competence follows the horizon, not the rule: an arm with no sense pixel closer than 5 hops to an answer scores 0.157 (chance); an arm with 19 of 64 pixels inside 2 hops scores 0.883, with growth, rent, death and the three-factor rule identical in both. The failure is forward information transmission, **not** backward credit assignment. | active | [2026-08-22-0208](./entries/2026-08-22-0208-exp002-m0-built-m1-fails-on-depth.md) |
| L-014 | Undirected growth starves small targets in proportion to their share of sites, not their importance: a 3-site output cortex in a 1,024-site lattice received 19 of 5,258 edges. Any substrate growing its own wiring toward a small, functionally critical target needs a target-attractiveness term, or the readout is starved by geometry before learning is even consulted. | active | [2026-08-22-0208](./entries/2026-08-22-0208-exp002-m0-built-m1-fails-on-depth.md) |
| L-015 | An instrument must suspend the costs the system pays, not just the learning it does. A diagnostic that switched rewards off but left rent running decayed every weight below the death threshold mid-probe and faithfully reported a disconnected, information-free network — an artefact of its own duration. Sibling of L-008: there an instrument dimensioned by its own readings, here one that let the system decay while it watched. | active | [2026-08-22-0208](./entries/2026-08-22-0208-exp002-m0-built-m1-fails-on-depth.md) |
| L-016 | A homeostat is an activity *source*, not only a stability lever. Global homeostatic inhibition adapting toward a target sparsity regenerates activity from a completely silent substrate — inhibition falls without bound while the error term stays negative — reaching target from zero even with spontaneous firing off and the resting bias buried at −30 (0.0000 → 0.0179 → 0.1499 over 25k ticks; permanently 0.0000 with the homeostat off). The pre-registered "no-spontaneous-activity" control removed `pSpont` and nothing else, so it would have bootstrapped normally and been recorded as a surviving control while controlling nothing. **A control arm must be verified to actually remove the thing it names.** | active | [2026-08-22-0208](./entries/2026-08-22-0208-exp002-m0-built-m1-fails-on-depth.md) |
| L-017 | The shortest input→output path is a misleading summary of a grown substrate's depth: an arm whose *shortest* path was 1 hop had a median sense pixel 3 hops from an answer, and was written up as "depth removed entirely" before the per-pixel distribution was measured. Report the path-length distribution over every input site — design §8 asked for exactly that, and the first implementation quietly substituted the minimum. | active | [2026-08-22-0208](./entries/2026-08-22-0208-exp002-m0-built-m1-fails-on-depth.md) |
