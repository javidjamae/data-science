# Dark but touchable: the sun gates vision only, and the world gets a second sense

- **Entry:** `2026-08-27-0051-two-senses`
- **When:** 2026-08-27 00:51 CDT (fifth entry of one conversation; settles the design opened in [0023](./2026-08-27-0023-the-world.md) and corrected in [0040](./2026-08-27-0040-the-sun.md))

- **Kind:** theory / design decisions — **no code ran** (journal rule 6)
- **Code state:** git `aaba633`, tree dirty with this session's doc edits

## In plain words

Javid settled two things. **First**, world validation runs on 001. **Second**,
and much bigger: when the sun goes down, the squares stop being *visible* but
do not stop being *there* — the organism can still reach out and feel them.

That one sentence gives the world two senses that disagree. Vision is wide,
cheap and only works in daylight. Touch is narrow, slow and works always. The
same object therefore arrives through different channels depending on the hour,
and being competent at night on something learned in daylight requires having
bound the two together.

**This is the multi-sense test the project has claimed since `vision.md` was
written and has never actually had** — and it arrives not as a feature but as a
consequence of a sun.

## The decision, as stated [J]

> "We can do world validation with 001, ya."

> "But let's make a decision about what the 'sun' actually is on the world. Do
> the squares go dark (not visible to the eye) when the sun is down, but the AI
> can still touch and feel them? So they're there, but not visible, but still
> touchable? So we get into the idea of two senses working together, along with
> a time element, along with a mouth to 'speak' what it sees / hears?"

> "I mean, this is just the world, we can first build simple games that don't
> require all senses and don't need all capabilities of the world, but then we
> make them more and more advanced games and measure how the entity responds as
> we change between games and do things at different times of day."

## Why the split is the good part

**Vision is distal and conditional. Touch is proximal and unconditional.** That
is the real biological division, and holding it produces the following without
anything else being designed:

1. **A forced cross-modal binding problem.** Daylight: vision is wide and touch
   is redundant. Night: vision is gone and touch is the only channel — but it
   is narrow, so competence requires moving and integrating over time. To carry
   a daylight-learned discrimination into the dark, the organism must have
   bound *what this looks like* to *what this feels like*.
2. **The cleanest transfer test the project has ever had.** Learn it by eye in
   daylight, test it by hand at night, with no retraining. Compare against
   [L-029](../LEARNINGS.md): 003's "same outputs, different stimuli" turned out
   to be a **union**, and the corrected reversal test was **contradiction**.
   Cross-modal is a third thing and it is cleaner than either — **same
   stimulus, same answer, different channel.** Nothing about the knowledge
   changes; only the road it arrives on.
3. **A symmetry with [H-018](../HYPOTHESES.md) worth naming.** H-018 factors
   perception from *communication* — the output protocol changes and the
   knowledge stays. This factors knowledge from *modality* — the input channel
   changes and the knowledge stays. Same claim, opposite end of the organism.
4. **A difficulty gradient with no task redesign.** The identical game is
   harder at night, because touch imposes sequential integration and working
   memory where vision allowed a single parallel glimpse. The world supplies
   the curriculum instead of the experimenter.

## Decisions

**D1 — World validation runs on 001 [J].** And this is *not* an exception to
the [2026-08-22-2022 doctrine](./2026-08-22-2022-001-is-not-the-ipnn.md); it is
the doctrine applied correctly. The doctrine says 001 is a harness and control,
and validating that a world reproduces recorded behaviour is harness work.
**Scope, stated so it cannot drift:** 001 may be used to show the *world* is
correct. Any claim about the *organism* — learning, adaptation, transfer,
timing — runs on the grown substrate.

**D2 — The sun gates vision only. Touch is unconditional.** Objects remain
present and tangible in the dark.

**D3 — Darkness reads as emptiness, not as a distinct "unknown" symbol.** A
special unlit token would be the world *telling* the organism it cannot see —
[0040](./2026-08-27-0040-the-sun.md)'s error in a new place. Dark cells read
0, identical to genuinely empty ones, and the difference has to be *inferred*
from where the light is. Ambiguity is the honest version.

**D4 — Touch is one cell, at the hand's position.** Maximally narrow, which is
what forces movement and integration. Fallback to 3×3 only if 1-cell makes
every night game unsolvable rather than merely hard, and that fallback is
recorded as a deviation if taken.

**D5 — The hand enters as a *sense* before it enters as an *effector*.** Feel
is afferent, cheap and safe; write/erase is efferent and carries §B1's named
reward-hacking and closed-loop-instability risks. Splitting them means v0 gets
a second sense with no new mechanism, and manipulation waits behind §B1's
operant gate. This falls out of Javid's proposal and materially de-risks the
hand.

**D6 — Both eye and hand are positioned by the teacher in v0.** §G rung 1. No
motor, no efference copy, no new mechanism.

**D7 — Touch is available in daylight too, and this is load-bearing.** If touch
only worked at night the two channels would never co-occur and cross-modal
binding would be impossible *by construction*. The senses must overlap in
daylight or decision D2 buys nothing.

**D8 — Sun geometry.** The day is a cycle of phases: the sun occupies column
*i* and lights a band around it, advancing one column every T ticks across the
world, followed by N phases of no sun at all (night). Full-illumination mode
(sun everywhere, always) is the **default**, which makes the recorded-behaviour
control free and matches the repo's flag discipline — new mechanisms default to
the recorded behaviour.

## The ladder this produces

Each rung is a game, each names the null it must beat, and no rung needs a
mechanism the project does not have until rung 3.

| # | Game | Channels | Question | Null it must beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | `identify` (full light) | eye, mouth | **calibration** | **must match 001/003 recorded curves** |
| 1 | `look-around` | eye+pos, mouth | is the world bigger than the sense? | one-glimpse control must **fail** |
| 2 | `feel` | touch+pos, mouth | can it identify by touch alone? | shuffled touch positions |
| 3 | `see-then-feel` | eye → touch | **cross-modal transfer** | learned-by-touch-from-scratch control |
| 4 | `night-shift` | both, sun running | same game, day versus night | uniformly-lit world of matched difficulty |
| 5 | `when` | both, sun | does behaviour depend on phase? | constant-sun arm |
| 6 | `free-run` | both, sun removed | **does the rhythm persist?** ([H-032](../HYPOTHESES.md)) | never-entrained control |

**Rung 3 is the headline.** It is the first result on this ladder that would be
worth writing up, and it is the one the whole world design exists to make
askable.

## Prior art & novelty

- **Similar:** cross-modal object recognition — learn by one modality, test by
  another — is an established comparative paradigm run on rats, monkeys and
  human infants, and passes [H-009](../HYPOTHESES.md) without argument.
  Vision-versus-touch under changing illumination is the textbook multisensory
  setup. Partial observability under a moving illumination window is standard
  POMDP territory. Sun-compass navigation couples light to both time and
  direction in bees and birds.
- **Different:** the two senses are gated by a *world property the organism can
  perceive*, so the modality switch is neither announced nor arbitrary — the
  organism can in principle **anticipate** it.
- **Novel (claimed):** nothing. *Unverified against literature* — the fifth
  owed search from this session, and the unsearched backlog is now large enough
  to be a standing threat to the record rather than a footnote.

## Threats to validity

**The obvious confound, and it kills rung 3 if unhandled:** an organism that
learns "answer only when lit" produces perfectly time-correlated behaviour with
no timekeeping and no cross-modal binding in it whatsoever. Rung 4's
matched-difficulty uniformly-lit arm exists for this and must run before rung 3
is believed.

**Second:** D3 makes darkness and emptiness identical, which means a night
world is *literally* an empty visual field. If a game's visual answer is ever
"nothing is there", night and that answer are indistinguishable and the game is
broken. Games must be screened for this before they enter the ladder.

**Third, and structural:** this entry designs seven games and zero of them
exist. The [L-012](../LEARNINGS.md) pattern — building outward while the
mechanism stays unbuilt — is the failure mode with the highest prior here, and
rung 0's gate is the only thing standing against it.
