## In plain words

Yesterday's-hour finding: the organism's memory mechanism eventually kills its
ability to learn, because confidence only ever accumulates. The fix, promised
by the mechanism's own name: track evidence *for* and *against* each
connection separately, so a connection whose world has changed can become
uncertain — and plastic — again.

Built behind a switch, with the old rule kept as the default so every recorded
result and the published demo stay exactly what they were (verified to the
digit, not assumed). Three success bars written down and committed before
running.

Result: **the aging is cured, the fix missed its numeric bar anyway, and the
deepest question came back with a clean answer.**

- Cured: with the fix, nothing in the organism is frozen at the end of eight
  rule-flips — measured directly, every one of its 480 readout connections
  still movable. Rule-flips learnable went from 7 of 32 to 18 of 32.
- Missed: the bar said 20 of 32, to match what turning consolidation off
  entirely had achieved. 18 is not 20. The verdict recorded is FAIL.
- Answered: the failure mode we feared was that memory and flexibility are one
  dial — that you cannot keep remembering without eventually rusting shut. The
  retention test says no: with the fix, memory got slightly *better* (0.982
  against 0.952) while the rust was removed. Two dials, not one.

And one more wrong guess caught the same hour: the shortfall is *not* the fix
re-freezing (a connection "confidently wrong" is also one-sided). Measured:
zero re-frozen connections on both a stalled seed and a healthy one. The
remaining failures are the same ones the no-consolidation arm has — reversal
is just hard for this noisy learning rule — which is a different lever.

## Analysis

**The terminal aging of L-034 is gone, and that is a direct measurement, not
an inference from scores.** Under count, seeds that start failing never
recover. Under beta, seed 1 fails rev3 and then learns five of the next five;
seed 3 is down for six stages and comes back at rev8. There is no absorbing
state, because there is nothing frozen to absorb into — 480/480 synapses
plastic at end of life on every seed checked.

**The pre-registered bar was still missed, and the verdict stands as FAIL.**
18 < 20. The improvement (2.6×, and better on every other measure) does not
amend a bar that was set to "parity with consolidation off." Worth recording
why parity was not reached: the re-freeze hypothesis is refuted, and the
stalls line up with the no-consolidation arm's own failures (10 of 32) — this
is the learning rule's noisy credit assignment under contradiction
([L-030](../LEARNINGS.md)), not an evidence-model defect. The next lever for
reversal speed is the rule or the exploration schedule, not consolidation.

**Gate 2 is the finding with theoretical weight.** The pre-registered reading
of a retention failure was "memory and flexibility are one dial — L-004's
tension is fundamental." Retention instead *improved* (0.982 vs 0.952; count's
own seed-42 run reproduces L-005's 0.97 exactly). So at this scale the tension
is a property of the *count implementation*, not of consolidation as such: you
can keep consolidation, remove the terminal rigidity, and lose nothing.
Plausible mechanism for the improvement, offered as interpretation only: error
trials during acquisition land β on the synapses that drove the error, keeping
the *wrong* pathways plastic and correctable while the right ones consolidate.

**One more same-hour refutation, kept because it is evidence.** The re-freeze
guess ("β≫α is also one-sided, so contradiction eventually freezes the other
way") was the obvious mechanism for the shortfall and it is measurably absent.
Its absence is what licenses "the residual failures are
consolidation-independent."

## Learnings

- **L-036:** Tracking contradicting evidence separately (plasticity ∝
  1/(1+|α−β|/n₀)) **removes L-034's cumulative learning death**: 480/480
  synapses remain plastic after eight reversals (vs terminal freezing under
  count), reversal survival rises 7→18 of 32 — but the pre-registered parity
  bar (≥20, the no-consolidation reference) was missed, and the residual
  failures match the no-consolidation arm's own: they are the rule's noisy
  credit assignment under contradiction, not consolidation. *Evidence:* this
  entry.
- **L-037:** **Memory and flexibility are two dials at this scale, not one.**
  The α/β model removes the rigidity while frozen retention *improves* (0.982
  vs 0.952), refuting the "one dial" reading that a gate-2 failure would have
  supported. L-004's tension is real for the count model and absent for a
  model whose confidence can fall. *Evidence:* this entry, gates 1+2 jointly.

## Decisions

1. **`'count'` stays the default.** 001's published demo and every recorded
   result remain records of the certified rule, bit-identical (M1 curves
   verified against entry 2026-08-16-0248 after the change).
2. **Experiment 004 starts on `'beta'`.** It dominated count on every measure
   run today (reversals 18v7, retention 0.982v0.952, M1 tails 0.99v0.98), and
   004 should not inherit a substrate known to age to death. Recorded as a
   pre-launch decision, not a post-hoc one.
3. **No further consolidation tuning.** The shortfall's cause lies in the
   learning rule under contradiction, so pushing on n₀ or the evidence form
   would be knob-twiddling at the wrong knob.
4. 002's design §7 note ("registered as the first follow-up") is now
   discharged — the follow-up exists, with results.

## Threats to validity

1. **Four seeds, extreme per-stage variance** (589–2,466 on successful
   stages). The 18-vs-22 comparison in particular is well inside plausible
   seed noise, which cuts both ways: parity may in truth have been reached, or
   the gap may be real. The recorded verdict follows the bar, not the vibe.
2. **The re-freeze diagnostic used one threshold** (5·n₀) and two seeds.
3. **Retention improvement (+0.030) is small** against per-seed spread
   (count: 0.93–0.97); "improves" is safe, "improves by 3 points" is not.
4. **All of this is 001.** 002's grown substrate has its own copy of the count
   rule and inherits nothing automatically.
