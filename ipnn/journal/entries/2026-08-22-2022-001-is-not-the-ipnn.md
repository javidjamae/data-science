## The directive, in Javid's words [J]

> "001 is NOT the IPNN. It's using some old-school pre-defined architecture.
> The point of our neural net is that it is SELF LEARNING — the neurons
> themselves learn to connect to other neurons and create neural pathways.
> EVERY SINGLE THING you do moving forward has to follow that model. Every
> experiment — unless we're just testing out creating a simple harness for
> something. We need to go back to 002 and rethink the next experiments
> around that growth model. I want a neuron-growth model that converges on
> working."

Stated in the strongest terms, after a full day in which the experiment queue
(003, then the 004 and 005 designs) repeatedly landed on 001 because 001 was
the substrate that demonstrably learned.

## Why the drift happened, named honestly

Each individual choice was locally defensible — "test the doctrine on the
substrate that can learn", "cheapest informative experiment" — and the sum of
them was a day of measuring a hand-designed architecture the project's own
record says is not the point (L-010: its designed structure earns nothing).
Optimizing for measurable results over the mission's substrate is a failure
mode, and it now has a name and three guards: this entry, the vision.md
amendment, and a persistent memory entry so no future session re-derives the
drift.

## What binds now

1. Every experiment, demo and organism-claim runs on a **grown** substrate.
   001 is a harness and labelled control arm only.
2. The standing priority is **a neuron-growth model that converges** — passes
   002's M1-class gate — attacked directly, starting with the knob space
   Javid wants to explore, then the diagnosed mechanisms (M1e beacon, H-006
   correlation growth, H-012 scaffolding, the α/β port).
3. 004 and 005 keep their pre-registrations but are **parked** until a grown
   substrate can host them.
