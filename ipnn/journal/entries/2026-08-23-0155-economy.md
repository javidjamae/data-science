## In plain words

Two landlord rules were added to save the inherited highways the organism was
bulldozing: **earned durability** (a road that carries traffic becomes cheap
to maintain) and a **juvenile grace period** (no bulldozing anything young).

The clean result: **durability alone is the fix, and grace backfires.**

With durability only, all three organisms show the first *sustained, rising*
learning this geometry has ever produced — curves climbing from chance to
0.59–0.64 and still rising at the end, where every previous configuration
either sat dead or decayed. And it wins while keeping only ~10% of the
inherited routes: the ones that earn. The rest die, as they should.

Grace, in this form, floods the city: every rewiring dumps more rent-free
juvenile junk (edge counts triple to ~17k), congesting the sheet — and the
combined arm is *worse* than durability alone. Selection must never be
switched off, even for childhood — L-027's lesson, relearned at the
schedule level.

The reproduction check passed: the fixes-off arm reproduces the recorded
2103 phenomenon at HEAD (same birth routes 20/29/18, same bulldozing to
1/0/0 by trial 1,500).

## Against the pre-registered gates, honestly

- **RETENTION (w2end ≥ 50% of w2birth on `both`): FAIL** — every arm loses
  most raw routes (best retention ~10–25%).
- **TRAJECTORY (last block ≥ first on `both`): FAIL on `both`** (seed 3 flat
  at 0.33, seed 1 decays late). **PASS on `rent-only`, all three seeds**
  (0.34→0.48, 0.28→0.64, 0.24→0.59).
- **STRETCH (M1 gate 0.8): not reached** — best tail 0.64.

The gates were registered on the combined arm and the combined arm failed
them; the single-mechanism arms — run precisely for attribution — are what
exposed durability as the carrier and grace as the saboteur. The retention
gate also turns out to have measured the wrong conserved quantity: what must
survive is not the inheritance but the *earners*, and ~2–3 earning routes
out of 20–29 were enough to lift three seeds off the floor.

## Learnings

- **L-043:** **Earned durability alone produced the first sustained rising
  learning on 002's hard geometry** — tails 0.48/0.64/0.59 with curves still
  climbing, versus 0.19-and-dead baseline — while retaining only the earning
  ~10% of innate routes. The conservation target is *earners, not
  inheritance*: informative rent (L-027) plus use-dependent durability is
  selection that keeps winners, and that suffices where whole-scaffold
  retention gates were aiming at the wrong thing. H-023's one-line form is
  **supported**. *Evidence:* this entry.
- **L-044:** **Rent-free childhood backfires.** Grace as implemented lets
  every rewiring deposit junk that pays nothing and cannot die, tripling the
  edge population (~17k) and congesting the sheet; the combined arm
  underperforms durability alone on 2 of 3 seeds. Selection must not be
  suspended, even temporarily and even for juveniles — L-027 at the schedule
  level. H-022's smallest form is **refuted as implemented**; the idea
  (schedules) stays open in selection-preserving forms. *Evidence:* this
  entry, grace-only and both arms.

## Decisions

1. **`rentN0: 25` + the long innate scaffold is the new working
   configuration of 002** — labelled honestly: *not from zero* (seedEdges
   reported per the design's own caveat), and *not yet at the 0.80 gate*.
2. **Grace is withdrawn** in its rent-free form. Any future schedule must
   keep juveniles under selection (e.g., reduced-not-zero rent).
3. **Next, per the pre-registered decision rule adapted to what happened:**
   the economy now keeps earners, so the kill-and-mutate scaffold loop
   (H-014, Javid's proposal) runs on the durable economy — selection over
   scaffolds finally optimizes computation, not rent-survival.
4. The demo gains a **Durable** arm (published, same URL) so the working
   version is watchable: live-verified at 0.65 rolling after 5,285 trials on
   seed 2.

## Threats to validity

1. Three seeds; seed-level variance remains large (0.48–0.64).
2. `rentN0 = 25` untuned — one value, chosen by rough argument.
3. 2,500 trials; whether the rising curves reach the 0.80 gate with more
   life is unknown (the demo run reached 0.65 at 5,285 trials, suggesting a
   slow climb, n=1).
4. The scaffold remains random and unmutated; all results are lower bounds
   on what selection over scaffolds could reach.
