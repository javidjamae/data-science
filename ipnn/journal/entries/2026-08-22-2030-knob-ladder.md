## In plain words

Javid's call: stop everything else and make 002 — the substrate that grows its
own wiring — actually work, starting with a serious pass over its knobs. Fifty
deliberate variations were run, grouped by which diagnosed failure they attack:
louder synapses, denser wiring, cheaper rent, different excitability, wider
growth reach, longer credit horizons, and the promising combinations.

**Not one of the fifty moved the thing that matters.** The column to read is
`within2` — how many of the 64 eye pixels end up within two connections of an
answer neuron. It is **0/0 on every arm**. All fifty organisms, whatever their
knobs, grew bodies in which every path from eye to answer is 4+ hops — and
L-013 says information survives exactly one. Every arm was information-dead by
construction, and their accuracies (best worst-seed 0.30, gate 0.80) are just
different flavours of that same death.

One symptom DID respond to knobs: the starved readout (L-014). Bigger birth
weights and degree caps took edges-reaching-an-answer from 19 to 70. It
changed nothing, because those edges carry noise. **A knob can make the wire
thicker; it cannot make the wire carry signal.**

The pre-registered prediction held and the pre-registered decision rule fires:
knobs alone are insufficient at this geometry. The next build is mechanisms —
M1e's output beacon, H-006's correlation-seeking growth, H-012's scaffolding,
and the α/β port.

## Learnings

- **L-040:** **Knobs move symptoms, not structure.** Fifty hypothesis-grouped
  variations of 002's parameter space (SNR, density, economy, excitability,
  fields, rule, combos), on the real M1 geometry: zero arms put a single
  sense pixel within two hops of an answer, and none exceeded a worst-seed
  tail of 0.30 against a 0.80 gate. The one knob-responsive symptom — readout
  starvation, 19→70 edges — was worthless while the paths carry noise. With
  the 327-draw random sweep, the parameter space is now adequately explored:
  002's failure is mechanistic, and only mechanism changes can fix it.
  *Evidence:* this entry, plus the sweep record.

## Decisions

1. **The knob phase is closed** (this ladder + the 327-draw sweep). Per the
   pre-registered rule: the next build is the four diagnosed mechanisms, in
   the order M1e beacon (cheapest) → H-006 correlation growth (aimed at the
   wall) → H-012 scaffolding → α/β port, each behind a flag, each with a
   pre-registered measure, singles before combos.
2. The L-021 depth-carrying positive control gets built alongside, before any
   "information now crosses hops" claim.

## Threats to validity

1. Screen was 2 seeds × 1,000 trials — coarse, and a knob combination that
   only shines late or on other seeds would be missed. The uniformity of
   `within2 = 0` across all 50 is the finding, and it is not a seed effect.
2. Fifty arms is not exhaustive; it is fifty *chosen* points plus 327 random
   ones. "Adequately explored" is a judgment, recorded as such.
