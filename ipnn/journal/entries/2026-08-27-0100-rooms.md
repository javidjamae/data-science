## In plain words

Two entries ago the world got a sun. Javid has now generalised it: **the
primitive is not a sun, it is light** — and the unit is not a world, it is a
**room**. Different rooms have different lighting: one has a sun crossing it,
one is simply lit, one is dark, one has a strobe, one has light coming in from
a single side that fades out after a few squares. All are legitimate places to
be, and the organism can be put in any of them.

The consequence that matters most is not aesthetic. **Whether time is knowable
becomes a property of the room.** In a sunlit room you can tell what hour it
is. In a lit windowless room you cannot — the same way a person in a basement
office cannot, no matter how well lit it is. Which means the decisive
chronobiology experiment stops being something the experimenter *does* to the
world and becomes somewhere the organism *goes*.

## The idea, as stated [J]

> "Another approach would be to think about the 'sun' as being light, and we
> could have different 'rooms' rather than 'worlds' and different rooms have
> different light properties. So going between games could involve different
> 'suns' or different light properties. The light could be a strobe that moves
> around. It could be LIGHTS ON. It could only have an intensity that shines in
> from one side and lasts 3 squares or 10 squares. So light can have intensity,
> and entities in the room are just what's drawn on the grid and the light
> illuminates them. So for some rooms, the sun coming and going creates an
> effect of time. But in other rooms when the light is on, there is not a
> displayed show of time, just like if you were sitting in an enclosed lit (or
> dark) room and had no sun to indicate the time. All are valid 'rooms' to be
> in. And the entity can be exposed to any/all of these rooms."

## What the generalisation buys

**1. Free-running becomes a place, not an intervention [J].** Chronobiology's
constant-conditions protocol requires the experimenter to freeze the world.
Here you walk into a windowless room. [H-032](../HYPOTHESES.md)'s test —
entrain, remove the zeitgeber, watch whether the rhythm persists and drifts —
is now a *transition between two rooms that both exist for their own reasons*.
That is a strictly better experiment: nothing is switched off, nothing is
special-cased, and the organism is never handled.

**2. Light carries time *or* space *or* neither, and the room chooses.** A
light that **moves** carries time. A light with a **gradient** carries space —
"it is bright on my left and fades over ten squares" locates you without any
motor system or efference copy. A uniform static light carries neither and is
the honest control for both. One primitive, two orthogonal kinds of structure,
and the ability to have either without the other is what makes them separable
experimentally.

**3. It kills a hardcode.** [§I](../../experiment-ideas.md) exists because *the
contract had no owner* and every experiment reinvented its world. "The world
has a sun" would have been the same mistake one level up. A room is a
container — contents, a light, a game — and the sun is a value.

## The decision I owe: what intensity means on a binary substrate [J→C]

Javid's light has **intensity**, and the eye reads binary pixels. Two ways to
reconcile that, and only one is admissible:

- **Make the eye graded.** Changes the sense interface, and every recorded
  result stops reproducing. Rejected on the repo's standing rule.
- **Intensity is the probability that an illuminated cell's pixel fires this
  tick.** 1.0 = certain, 0.0 = dark, between = noisy perception.

**The second, and it is not merely the safe option — it is the one that fits.**
The substrate is *already* stochastic; probabilistic sparse firing is in
`abstract.md` §2 and in the code. Light intensity riding on firing probability
costs nothing, changes no interface, and makes full illumination (p=1.0
everywhere) *exactly* the recorded behaviour, so [0051](./2026-08-27-0051-two-senses.md)'s
D8 default falls out rather than being asserted.

And it has a consequence worth more than the tidiness: **a dim room is the
first task the project has ever had where looking longer should pay.**
[L-012](../LEARNINGS.md) recorded that iterative consideration — the mechanism
this project is *named for* — has never been built, and one honest reason is
that no task existed where it would help; every stimulus was fully legible in
one glance. Dim light creates evidence accumulation as a *requirement* rather
than a hypothesis, which is what §A3 ("does thinking longer help?") has been
missing. Registered as [H-035](../HYPOTHESES.md).

## The room contract

A **room** is: dimensions · contents (what is drawn) · a **light** · a game.

A **light** is: source geometry (none · uniform · point/column · directional
from an edge) × motion (static · moving with period T · strobe) × intensity
(peak level, falloff over *d* squares).

The initial named set — **enumerated, not swept**, for the reason below:

| Room | Light | Carries time? | Carries space? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bright` | uniform, full, static | no | no |
| `dark` | none | no | no |
| `dim` | uniform, low intensity | no | no |
| `windowless` | uniform, full, static | **no** — deliberately identical to `bright`, named separately because its *role* is the constant-conditions arm | no |
| `sunlit` | moving band, wraps, night phase | **yes** | weakly |
| `sidelit` | directional from an edge, falloff *d* | no | **yes** |
| `strobe` | uniform, on/off period | **yes**, crudely | no |

That table is most of the experiment design already:

- `bright` → `dark` — cross-modal transfer (0051's rung 3)
- `sunlit` → `windowless` — **free-running, as a place** (H-032)
- `bright` → `dim` — does iteration compensate? (H-035, §A3)
- `sidelit` — a spatial reference frame from a gradient alone, no motor (§G)

## Decisions

1. **§I is reframed: one body, many rooms.** Light is the primitive; the sun is
   an instance. The track's artefact is the room contract.
2. **Intensity = firing probability.** The eye stays 64 binary pixels; the sense
   interface never changes.
3. **Rooms are enumerated, never swept.** [L-040](../LEARNINGS.md) is explicit:
   fifty knob variations moved symptoms and not one pixel of structure. A
   light with geometry × motion × intensity × falloff is a knob space, and a
   knob space is where an infrastructure project goes to die. A fixed set of
   *named* rooms, each existing because a specific game needs it, is the
   discipline. Adding a room requires naming the game that could not be played
   without it.
4. **H-034, H-035 registered.**

## Prior art & novelty

- **Similar:** procedurally varied environments with held-out configurations is
  the Procgen/GVGAI/XLand pattern. Illumination-varying perception is standard
  in robotics and vision. Constant-conditions free-run is Aschoff/Pittendrigh.
  Graded evidence under sensory noise is drift-diffusion (Ratcliff; Gold &
  Shadlen) — already in `related-work.md` for the A-track.
- **Different:** *time-legibility as a property of the environment* rather than
  of the protocol, so entrainment and free-running are locations rather than
  experimenter actions.
- **Novel (claimed):** nothing. *Unverified* — sixth owed search from this
  session.

## Threats to validity

**The room space is a knob space wearing a costume**, and this project has
already lost a full day to exactly that shape ([L-040](../LEARNINGS.md)).
Decision 3 is the guard and it is a discipline, not a mechanism — it will hold
only as long as someone enforces "name the game that needs it."

**Second:** `windowless` and `bright` are physically identical and differ only
in the experimenter's intent. That is fine as a control but it is *also* the
kind of distinction that quietly becomes a story about the organism when the
data is ambiguous. The two must be run as genuinely blind arms.

**Third:** intensity-as-probability means a dim room and a *noisy* room are the
same thing to the organism. If a later game wants noise and dimness to differ,
this mapping forecloses it. Recorded now so it is a known limitation rather
than a later surprise.
